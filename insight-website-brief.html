Before the pixels: a better website brief.

The most useful website brief does not start with colours. It starts with the change you want the website to make.

WEBORA FIELD NOTES · 3 MIN READ
01

Choose one primary action

Should a visitor book a call, enquire about a service, or explore a product? Decide which action matters most. Secondary journeys can still exist, but the main path needs a clear priority.

02

Describe the visitor’s questions

Write down what someone needs to know before contacting you: what you do, who it is for, what the process looks like, and what makes your approach relevant. This is the beginning of your page structure.

03

Collect proof, not decoration

Gather real project images, clear descriptions of your role, and results you can substantiate. If you are starting out, label concept work honestly and explain the thinking behind it.

04

Agree the practical boundaries

List the pages, required features, content responsibilities, budget range, and target timeline. A shared understanding now gives design and development a much clearer direction.
