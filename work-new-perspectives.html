SELF-INITIATED / NOT CLIENT WORK

The idea
behind it.

A self-initiated map illustration showing how a neighbourhood can become a distinctive visual asset. This is a fictional place, not a surveyed location.

The creative challenge

Give viewers a sense of place while keeping the important landmarks and destination easy to recognise.

The direction

An isometric perspective, a limited palette, and a contrasting marker organise the view. In a real project, the location references and essential details would guide the final illustration.

Concept artwork only. No client affiliation, live campaign, or commercial performance is claimed.
