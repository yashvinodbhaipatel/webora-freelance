The idea
behind it.

A self-initiated campaign world for a fictional botanical drink. Bright colour and a simple product story create a direction that can stretch across social formats.

The creative challenge

Make an everyday product feel recognisable in a crowded feed without relying on a long explanation.

The direction

Citrus tones, playful type, and a tilted product composition create a compact visual language. A commissioned campaign would then define the audience, claims, production assets, and measurement plan.

Concept artwork only. No client affiliation, live campaign, or commercial performance is claimed.
