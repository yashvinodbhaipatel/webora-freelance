Your project brief

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp link containing the details you enter. When you continue, those details are passed to WhatsApp so you can review and send the message. This website does not save your form details to a server.

External services

WhatsApp and Instagram links take you to services with their own privacy policies. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; that request exposes standard connection information, such as your IP address, to the font provider.

Cookies and preferences

No analytics or advertising tracking is included in this website’s source. The motion toggle saves your preference in this browser using local storage. A hosting provider may process standard access logs.
