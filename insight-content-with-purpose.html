Give every piece of content a job.

A beautiful feed is useful when it also helps people understand your brand. Give each content idea a purpose before deciding on the format.

WEBORA FIELD NOTES · 3 MIN READ
01

Introduce, explain, or invite

Some posts introduce the brand. Others explain a product or answer a question. Others invite the viewer to take a next step. Choose one main job for each idea so the message stays focused.

02

Start with the product truth

What is useful, surprising, or distinctive about the product? Build the story around a detail you can actually show. A clear demonstration often communicates more than a list of broad claims.

03

Let the format follow the idea

A quick demonstration might work as a short video; a process might need a sequence of images. Choose the format that makes the idea easiest to understand, then adapt it for the platform.

04

Review what the content taught you

Compare the response with the original purpose. Questions, saves, relevant enquiries, and viewing patterns can each tell a different story. Use that information to decide what to make next.
